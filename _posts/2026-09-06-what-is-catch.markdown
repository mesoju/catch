`Welcome to the website for CATCH!`

**Okay,** you're probably wondering, *what the actual freak is CATCH* and why is there such a cool website for it (I know right)? 

Well, as you know `FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC)` isn't exactly the easiest thing in the world to do at all. It's complicated, and matter of fact I think it's something that you wouldn't fully get to "experience" until you're in it for at least 2 years. But I want **FRC** to be accessible. I want as many people to experience it as in depth as I got to experience it alongside the majority of the current executive members. This is what `CATCH!` tries to solve. 

Currently, **CATCH!** is a pilot-program that only **Programming** and **Electrical** has agreed to try out, but I will gradually refine it and build it into something more meaningful, and hopefully expand to the entire Robotics team. The main point is that, well, a lot of the executives this year including myself are *seniors*, and we're trying to keep this club alive even when we might not be around as often anymore. **CATCH** is an opportunity for me to create videos and articles that provides a wealth of knowledge to newer members who can benefit from it, and hopefully the information doesn't become dated too soon. I plan to join **Tech's** Robotics Outreach program in order to continue to provide insight into FRC as well as continue to mentor even after I am in college.

`YES, I am THAT committed to Robotics`, and I think you will eventually become just as committed! Robotics is an extremely valuable extracurricular in high school that not many realize; it provides work and hands-on experience while also giving opportunities to travel and escape school (ain't that so fun? :p). I want to try and shoot for `WORLDS` this year, not only would it look extremely good for me as this program's director, but colleges will also eventually see this on **YOUR application** and it speaks VOLUMES. 

There's just way too much to discuss about FRC in such a little amount of time:
1. Review and Strategize the Season's Game (Strategy)
2. Design and Manufacture the Robot (Fabrication)
3. Assemble and Maintain the Robot (Mechanical)
4. Wire and Connect the Robot Systems (Electrical)
5. Program and Operate the Robot (Programming)
6. Present and Demonstrate our IMPACT. (Impact)

All of these need to be done within 3 months after kick-off, and we're **only** meeting for 2 hours every week on Wednesdays? <br>
**CATCH** resolves this by providing articles and videos that are 'slightly' more fun to watch and catered personally to our team. Research and understanding how to work with FRC has historically been the hardest part of Robotics, but with **CATCH** we do the hard part by reading through all documentation, previous ideas and strategies, and concepts, and then compressing these ideas into bite-sized articles and videos that you can watch and not have to stress over. This website also serves as a 'hub' for documenting meeting notes and important links; think about this place as your go-to shop for accessing FRC-related material, at least that's my intent! 

Remember, `do not be afraid to ask for help from me or any of the executives when you need help or anything in general.` My goal is that you experience FRC Competitions without the extra stress, and that you are successful in being able to carry on this team. Historically, academic stress and the need to study has also been a huge factor in preventing us from committing to Robotics as much as we would have liked; I am qualified in **Math, History, Physics, and Computer Science** to be able to help you with the majority of school. I've taken about the hardest classes that the school has had to offer.
